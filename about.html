TuneVis

About

Music, visualised.

TuneVis is an interactive music recommender. Pick any song and see what it's made of: how danceable, energetic or acoustic it is. Then discover tracks that genuinely sound like it, and keep track of your own favourites with rankings and star ratings.

What you can do

Explore a song's sound

Search for any track to open its audio-features dashboard: danceability, energy, valence (how happy it sounds), speechiness, acousticness, instrumentalness and tempo, shown as interactive charts.

Find songs that sound alike

TuneVis gathers songs related to your track, then scores each one by how closely its audio features match. Compare them on a radar chart, a similarity bar chart and a scatter plot, and open any of them to keep exploring.

Rank what you love

Rank an album's tracks, an artist's top tracks or their albums, or build all-time favourite artist and track lists. Give albums a star rating out of five, in half-star steps.

Keep it all in one place

With a free TuneVis account you can save your rankings, sort them by date or stars, organise them into folders like "Favourites" or "2026 Releases", and pick up your recently viewed tracks on any device.

How the recommendations work

Each song is described by six audio features measured on a 0–1 scale. For every candidate song, TuneVis measures how far each of its features is from the song you picked, averages those distances, and turns the result into a similarity score: 100% means the two songs have near-identical features, 0% means they couldn't be more different.

Candidates come from songs listeners connect with your track. When none are available, TuneVis falls back to popular songs by similar artists. Some songs, often very new or older catalogue tracks, don't have audio features yet; when that happens you'll see a note instead of the charts.

Where the data comes from

  • Spotify Web API: song, artist and album details, artwork, search, and the embedded player.
  • ReccoBeats: the audio features behind every chart and similarity score.
  • Last.fm: trending charts, genre charts and related songs and artists.

TuneVis plays music through Spotify's embedded player. If a track won't play, sign in to Spotify in another tab or in the Spotify app, then try again.
