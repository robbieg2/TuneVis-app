Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

TuneVis is built to collect as little about you as possible. You can use it without an account, there are no adverts or tracking, and we never sell your data. This policy explains exactly what is stored, where, and how to delete it.

What we collect

If you don't have an account

Nothing about you is stored on our servers. Your searches go directly from your browser to the music services listed below. Your browser keeps a few small items so the site works smoothly (see Cookies and browser storage).

If you create an account

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password).
  • Your password, which is stored only in a securely hashed form by our authentication provider. We can never see it.
  • Your username, if you choose one. For now only you can see it.
  • What you save: your rankings (their names, the songs, albums or artists in them and their order), album star ratings, and the folders you organise them into.
  • Recently viewed: the last 10 songs you opened while signed in, so the list follows you between devices.

We don't collect your name, location, payment details or anything from your Spotify account. You don't sign in to TuneVis with Spotify, and TuneVis can't see or change your Spotify account.

How we use it

Only to run TuneVis: signing you in, saving and showing your rankings, keeping your recently viewed tracks, and sending account emails you ask for. We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we don't sell or share it with anyone for marketing.

Under UK and EU data protection law, our lawful basis is that this processing is necessary to provide the service you signed up for.

Services we rely on

TuneVis uses these providers to work. Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved data (email, hashed password, username, rankings, ratings, folders, recently viewed) and sends account emails.
  • Vercel hosts the website. Like any web host, it processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages.
  • Spotify provides song, artist and album details and the embedded player. Your searches are sent to Spotify's API. When you use the player, Spotify may set its own cookies.
  • ReccoBeats provides audio features for the songs you open.
  • Last.fm provides charts and related songs and artists for the songs you open.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the logo font and the sign-in library, so they receive your IP address when your browser downloads them.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and browser storage

TuneVis itself doesn't set any cookies and doesn't use tracking or advertising technology. It keeps a few items in your browser's local storage, all needed for the site to work:

  • your sign-in session, if you're signed in;
  • a short-lived access key that lets your browser look up songs on Spotify (it isn't linked to you);
  • temporary copies of chart results, so pages load faster;
  • a copy of your recently viewed tracks while you're signed in;
  • how you like your saved rankings sorted.

You can clear these at any time through your browser's settings. Signing out removes your session and recently viewed copy. Spotify's embedded player may set cookies of its own; see Spotify's cookie policy.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Recently viewed is limited to your last 10 songs. When you delete your account, your account and everything saved with it are removed from our database straight away. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it's used. You can do most of this yourself:

  • change your username or password on your account page;
  • delete individual rankings and folders on the Rankings page;
  • delete your whole account, and everything saved with it, from your account page.

For anything else, such as a copy of your data, contact us. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

TuneVis isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly create accounts for them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

The site is only served over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Database rules ensure each account can only ever read and change its own data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how your data is used, we'll make it clear on the site.
