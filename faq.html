Exploring songs

How is the similarity score worked out?

Every song has six audio features on a 0–1 scale: danceability, energy, valence, speechiness, acousticness and instrumentalness. TuneVis measures the gap between your song and each recommendation on all six, averages the gaps, and turns that into a percentage. 100% means near-identical features; 0% means as different as possible. Tempo is shown on the charts but isn't part of the score.

What do the audio features mean?

Danceability: how suitable the rhythm is for dancing. Energy: how intense and active it feels. Valence: how positive or happy it sounds. Speechiness: how much of it is spoken word. Acousticness: how likely it is to be acoustic rather than electronic. Instrumentalness: how likely it is to have no vocals. Tempo: its speed in beats per minute. The "?" button on any song's page has the full guide.

Why does a song say its audio features are coming soon?

Audio features come from ReccoBeats, which doesn't have every song yet, especially brand-new releases and some older catalogue. TuneVis tries a few other versions of the same song first; if none have features, it shows a note instead of the charts. Try again later, or pick another version of the song.

Why do I only hear a preview, or nothing at all?

The built-in player is Spotify's embedded player. It plays full songs only when you're signed in to Spotify in the same browser; otherwise it plays a short preview. To hear the whole song, either sign in at spotify.com in another tab, or tap Open in Spotify to play it in the Spotify app. Some tracks are also unavailable in certain countries.

Where do trending tracks and recommendations come from?

Trending tracks and genre charts come from Last.fm, as do the songs and artists related to the one you're exploring. Song details and artwork come from Spotify, and audio features from ReccoBeats.

Rankings

What can I rank?

An album's tracks (top 5 or the whole album), an artist's top tracks or their albums, and your all-time favourite artists or tracks. Search for an artist or album on the Rankings page, or choose "Create your own personal music rankings".

How do star ratings work?

Album rankings can have an overall rating from half a star to five stars. Tap the left half of a star for a half star, or the right half for a whole one. You can save an album with just a rating, before ranking any tracks.

How do I edit a saved ranking?

Open it from "Your saved rankings" on the Rankings page. It opens as a finished list; press Edit ranking to change it, then Done to save your changes and go back to the list.

How do folders work?

On the Rankings page, use "+ New folder" to create one, then the folder button on any saved ranking to add it. A ranking can be in more than one folder. Deleting a folder never deletes the rankings inside it.

Can I share a ranking?

Yes: press Export on any ranking to download it as an image or copy it as text, ready to post or send.

Your account

Do I need an account?

No. You can explore songs and build rankings without one. A free account lets you save rankings, ratings and folders, choose a username, and keep your recently viewed tracks across devices.

I didn't get my confirmation or password reset email

Check your spam or junk folder, and that you typed your email address correctly. The links expire after a while; if yours has, request a new one from the sign-in page.

How do I change my username or password?

Go to your account page (the round button in the top-right corner when you're signed in). You can change your username and password there.

How do I delete my account?

On your account page, open "Delete account" and type your email address to confirm. Your account, saved rankings, ratings, folders and recently viewed history are deleted straight away. This can't be undone. See the Privacy policy for more.

Everything else

Can I add TuneVis to my phone's home screen?

Yes. On iPhone, open TuneVis in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app". It then opens full screen like an app.

How do I listen to full songs from the home-screen app?

The home-screen app runs in its own browser, so (especially on iPhone) it isn't signed in to Spotify and the player plays previews. Tap Open in Spotify on any song, album or artist: it opens straight in the Spotify app, where it plays in full. Switch back to TuneVis whenever you like; it stays where you left it.

Is TuneVis connected to Spotify?

TuneVis uses Spotify's public Web API for song details and Spotify's embedded player for playback, but it's an independent project that isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify. You don't sign in to TuneVis with Spotify, and TuneVis can't see or change your Spotify account.

Does TuneVis cost anything?

No, TuneVis is free to use.
